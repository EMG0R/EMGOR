## Decisions applied
- **Cells (2026-11-12): Molicel P45B x8 (4 in use + 4 spares), authorized vendor only, not Amazon.** External charging only; one XTAR VC8-class 8-bay charger charges a full spare set (2-bay dropped). No charger board, no fuse.
- **Both instruments run 2 cells in parallel (1S2P)** in 2 single-cell spring holders each. Swap rules: power off, swap the pair together, matched pairs, keyed bays.
- **Wireless TX powered from the instrument battery** (5 V boost rail through a low-noise LDO), stereo pair of 2 TX per instrument at ~0.75 W each (estimate). Hat LEDs: one BTF FCOB WS2812B strip, 3.2 ft, 160 LED/m (~157 px), firmware cap ~20/255, with a MOSFET/switch on the strip's 5 V. Trumpet: no strip.
- Per instrument: 1S PCM >=5 A multi-FET, Pololu U3V40F5 5 V (verified 5 V), MAX17048, hard switch >=3 A on B+, switched 3.5 mm stereo jack. **No panel USB-C breakout, no LM66200 OR (2026-11-12):** the board's own USB (PB2 USB-C on the Gem, Seed 3 USB) is exposed through the housing with a printed strain-relief cradle and a right-angle/slim USB-C plug opening; USB powers the board only (Seed: onboard USB + VIN both safe on the original Seed, Seed 3 [U]), LEDs/sensors/TX stay on the boost.
- **Ferrite rule:** no ferrite or inductor in any ground/return path; ferrites allowed in series on supply rails only.
- Holders: SINGLE 21700 holders only (never 2-slot series). **Trumpet 2 cells (1S2P), hat 2: 2 holders each.** Contact mics and their buffer removed (v1 never used them). NeoPixel strips, seesaw driver and 74AHCT125 dropped from the trumpet.
- Mics: electret bias + low-noise op-amp preamp on protoboard, cable soldered direct, shield grounded at the PREAMP end only. MAX9814 rejected (AGC).
- Owned/excluded: Daisy Seed 3 (hat), NeoTrellis x2, headset mic. Trumpet buys one more Seed 3. Bela Gem Stereo no longer used (attic).
- Hat controls: analog joystick (X/Y + button) + NeoTrellis; 2 buttons + 1 encoder removed.

## Rows still NON-Amazon (and why)
- Molicel P45B x8 (kept off Amazon by decision: fake risk; authorized vendor)
- Pololu U3V40F5 (Amazon only lists under-current Pololu boosts; need >=2 A out at 3.3 V in)

## Amazon-risk flags
- **Fake/misrated risk on Amazon:** N52 magnets (grade), 1S "10 A" PCMs (bench-load it), op-amps OPA1612/OPA2134 (unfamiliar sellers), LT3045 modules (generic), XTAR/Nitecore chargers (buy Sold by brand), ADS1115 lookalike ICs.
- **Spec trades from Amazon-only:** SS49E ~3x lower sensitivity than DRV5055A2; WM-61A lower SNR/SPL than EM272; OPA1612 (bipolar) instead of OPA1662; seesaw ATtiny816 breakout instead of NeoDriver (address/NeoPixel pin to confirm).
- **Open design items:** op-amp needs >=4.5 V (do not use the Seed 3V3); WM-61A max SPL for the bell mount; LDO adjustability to 4.5 V.

## Suggested wireless (optional)
Not in the order. Written as an open-source suggestion for builders.

- **Provisional lead: Boss WL-20L (Emory is researching a replacement unit; do not treat this as the pick).** Boss's FAQ says WL-20L suits active-pickup, preamp-equipped and aerophone/line-level sources; WL-20 adds a cable-tone roll-off meant for passive guitars. 2.3 ms latency, 14 systems per space, ~50 ft. Amazon (from search results): WL-20L B07F2PY7DD, WL-20 B07F2NZWY5, spare TX WL-T B07DVZH2LN (pages gave 503; price ~$200 per set [U], stock unconfirmed).
- **Stereo-pair approach:** the WL-20 family is mono (only tip/L is transmitted). Stereo = two matched sets of the same model, one per channel. Three stereo instruments = 6 sets (~$1,200); trumpet + hat = 4 sets (~$800). 14 systems per space leaves headroom for 6 links. A fixed latency offset between L/R can be delay-compensated in DSP; per-packet jitter cannot.
- **Level matching:** Seed 3 line out full scale is 1 Vrms single-ended / 2 Vrms differential at the TAC5242 chip (board-level [U]). WL-T max input is unpublished, so start with a ~-26 dB pad per TX (20 k series / 1 k shunt, or a 10 k trimmer), then trim up. Optional parts: 1/4 in mono panel jack per TX (WL-T is a plug-in unit), trimmers.
- **Requirement (2026-11-12): the TX must run continuously from external 5 V/USB power with its internal cell removed or bypassed.** The TX are powered from the instrument battery (the 5 V boost rail through a low-noise LDO), never their own internal battery. Each instrument carries a stereo pair of 2 TX, budgeted at about 0.75 W each (an estimate, 1.5 W per instrument; see the runtime table in `SHARED_POWER_KIT.md`).
- **Powering caveats:** the Boss WL-20 / WL-T has a non-removable battery and cannot run while charging (Boss FAQ), so it is a poor fit for this requirement unless bench-proven (unknown fuel-gauge/protection behavior when fed externally). Any candidate unit must pass the external-power requirement above on the bench first. The RX can run while charging, but USB adapter noise can drop audio. Keep TX antennas off brass (15-30 cm, perpendicular to tubing).
- **Alternatives:** Rode Wireless GO II 2-person kit in split mode (2 TX to 1 RX, TX1 = L, TX2 = R, matched latency by construction, max input -20 dBV so a pad is mandatory, ~$299 per stereo link); Boya BY-WM4 Pro K2 (~$100-130, latency unverified); Xvive U2 / Line 6 G10 as cheap mono pairs; Shure GLXD1+ pairs (~$1,000+) or Sennheiser EW-D (UHF, best through brass, ~$2,000+) for pro. Don't mix two 2.4 GHz families (reported dropouts).
- **Guitar:** future, 2 more WL-20L sets (~$400) plus the 6-ch wired path.
