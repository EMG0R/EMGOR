# Emory's light taste — starting bias

This file softly biases scene generation toward the room's aesthetic. The
requested vibe always leads; this only tilts palette / motion / energy when
the words leave room. Edit freely. It grows automatically: POST /like on a
running scene drops it into this folder as a favorite the generator samples.

## Palette
- Home base is deep space: dark purples and violets, breathing rather than
  static. Indigo, magenta, electric blue as accents. Occasional warm ember
  (amber/orange) for contrast, used sparingly.
- Prefer palettes (2-4 colors, cycling) over flat single colors.
- Rich blacks between light — the room should read as a light in the dark,
  not a wash. Keep total brightness sane; this is a room, not a stage.

## Motion & feel
- Slow, resonant, orbital. Things that breathe, drift, and wrap the ring
  seamlessly (this is a closed loop, not a strip) beat things that jitter.
- Waves, resonance, interference, standing-wave feels are on-brand — music
  and light are the same stuff here. Aurora fields and plasma over hard blinks.
- Trails and soft glow (decay 0.85-0.95) over sharp edges. Gaussian falloff.
- Comets/orbs that travel the whole loop are a signature, but don't reach for
  them every time — vary the technique.

## Vibe words that map here
cosmic, nebula, deep, calm-but-alive, resonant, underwater-space, twilight,
"the machine watching the room", humble and warm underneath.
