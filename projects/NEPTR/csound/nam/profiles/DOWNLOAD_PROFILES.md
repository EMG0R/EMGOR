Drop the `.nam` files directly into this folder.

All are full-rig captures (amp + cab baked in — no IR needed).

## Notes
- Profiles are loaded into RAM one at a time (background thread swap, ~10ms)
- Use Menu 8 on NEPTR to scroll through profiles in real time
- CC75/CC76 step ±1, CC71/CC72 jump ±10
- CC24 encoder controls wet level (0.0–1.0)
- You can add hundreds more — the library directory is scanned recursively
