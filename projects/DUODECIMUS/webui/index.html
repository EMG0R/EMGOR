Each jack sends its own CCs, so the two are told apart. A jack acting as a footswitch is a Butt.

Physical layout

Encoder numbering is aligned in firmware, so encoder 1 really is the top-left knob. Use this to arrange the tiles to match your panel: turn on assign mode, click a cell, then turn the encoder you want there.

NeoPixel indices are typed by hand. The twelve pixels are outputs — nothing about them appears in the MIDI stream. Count them along the data chain from pin 13 and enter each cell's pixel number in its LED box.
