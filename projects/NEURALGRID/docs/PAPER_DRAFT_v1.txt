TITLE

[NAME]: A Perceptual Coordinate Protocol for Live Interaction with
Interchangeable Sound Engines


ABSTRACT

We present [NAME], a communication protocol in which controllers and sound
renderers share only a perceptual coordinate space: seven axes grounded in
auditory modulation representations. A fast path carries raw performance
events at sub-10 ms latency; a slow path carries "shapes" -- trajectories of
distributions u(t), s(t) over the space -- exchanged on musical boundaries,
so model latency is structurally inaudible. The controller's model never
perceives audio: it predicts the performer, and its prediction uncertainty
becomes the renderer's explicit, performable freedom. Captured real-world
sounds become shape objects that can be geometrically mutated and
re-rendered by any engine speaking the protocol. We demonstrate [NAME] with
physical and digital grid controllers driving interchangeable renderers,
from pure DSP to neural synthesis, and report on its use in live
performance.
