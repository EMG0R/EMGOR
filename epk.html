bio

EMGOR (Emory Smith) is a Los Angeles digital luthier who builds his own instruments end to end and plays them.

Emory Smith, known as EMGOR, is a digital luthier and audio-visual programmer in Los Angeles. He builds his instruments end to end, from the DSP and firmware to the touch interfaces and 3D-printed bodies, and performs on them with guitar. At the center of his rig is NEPTR, a Csound effects brain on a Raspberry Pi running his own operating system. He studies music technology at CalArts, plays in the Machine Lab robot orchestra, and publishes everything open source.

Emory Smith, known as EMGOR, is a digital luthier and audio-visual programmer in Los Angeles. He builds his instruments end to end: the DSP, the microcontroller firmware, the touch interfaces, and the 3D-printed bodies.

At the center of his live rig is NEPTR, a Csound effects brain on a Raspberry Pi running his own audio-first operating system, DEMIURGE. Around it orbit a hex-pickup hyperGuitar, the PMOR pocket groovebox, the neuralGrid gesture surface, and the DUODECIMUS foot controller. All of it is published open source.

He works in Csound, ChucK, Max/MSP, Faust, Pure Data and SuperCollider, studies in the Music Technology program at CalArts, and plays in the Machine Lab robot orchestra. His research targets NIME, ICMC and ICSC. As a guitarist he plays surf rock and generative ambient.
