EMGOR

Emory Smith · Los Angeles

bio

Emory Smith, known as EMGOR, is a digital luthier and audio-visual programmer in Los Angeles. He builds his instruments end to end: the DSP, the microcontroller firmware, the touch interfaces, and the 3D-printed bodies.

At the center of his live rig is NEPTR, a Csound effects brain on a Raspberry Pi running his own audio-first operating system, DEMIURGE. Around it orbit a hex-pickup hyperGuitar, the PMOR pocket groovebox, the neuralGrid gesture surface, and the DUODECIMUS foot controller. All of it is published open source.

He works in Csound, ChucK, Max/MSP, Faust, Pure Data and SuperCollider, studies in the Music Technology program at CalArts, and plays in the Machine Lab robot orchestra. As a guitarist he plays surf rock and generative ambient.
