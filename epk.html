EMGOR

Emory Smith · Los Angeles · emorysmith02@gmail.com

bio

EMGOR (aka Emory Smith) is a producer, multi-instrumentalist and technologist. His practice lies between the creation of new instruments and the use of them to communicate with musicians and audiences.

  • Developing a hyperguitar and hypertrumpet (hyperinstrument — an acoustic instrument augmented by technology to advance the player’s expressive and technical range)
  • Developing open-source effect processors, controllers and portable instruments which integrate into one modular performance system
  • Practicing and performing using new performance systems to improve range of artistic expression

Musical InfluencesPrince, Mid-Air Thief, Roy Hargrove, John Coltrane, Flanafi, J Dilla, Erykah Badu, Labi Siffre

Technological InfluencesRichard Boulanger, OKHOUSECAT, Matthew Davidson, Ajay Kapur, Ge Wang, Perry Cook, Tod Machover, Ian McCurdy, Max Matthews

B.A., Berklee College of Music, 2025
M.F.A., Creative Computing, California Institute of the Arts, expected 2027
