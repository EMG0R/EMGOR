bio

EMGOR (aka Emory Smith) is a producer, multi-instrumentalist and technologist. His practice lies between creating new instruments and using them to communicate directly with audiences and musicians. He focuses on developing technique across a variety of instruments and carrying it between electronic and acoustic music.

Currently

Developing master’s thesis on the evolving practice of creating and performing with electro-acoustic instruments.

Touring California and the Northeast (Oct–Nov) playing songs from his new EP It Is, showcasing new instruments and performance techniques.

Musical InfluencesPrince, Mid-Air Thief, Roy Hargrove, Flanafi, John Coltrane, J Dilla, Erykah Badu, Chon, Labi Siffre, Nick Drake

Technological InfluencesRichard Boulanger, OKHOUSECAT, Matthew Davidson, Ajay Kapur, Rory Walsh, Ge Wang, Perry Cook, Tod Machover, Ian McCurdy, Max Matthews

B.A., Berklee College of Music, 2025
M.F.A., Creative Computing, CalArts, 2027
