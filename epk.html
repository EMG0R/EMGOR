bio

EMGOR (aka Emory Smith) is a producer, multi-instrumentalist and technologist. His practice lies between the creation of new instruments and the use of them to communicate with musicians and audiences. Particular emphasis on developing technique across acoustic instruments and bringing that to electronic music, vice versa

  • Developing a masters thesis on the evolving relationship between creating and performing with electro-acoustic instruments
  • Developing hyperinstruments (acoustic instruments augmented by technology to advance a player’s expressive and technical range)
  • Developing effect processors, controllers and portable instruments which integrate into one cohesive performance system
  • Practicing, performing and recording with these new systems to make unique, expressive music

Musical InfluencesPrince, Mid-Air Thief, Roy Hargrove, Flanafi, John Coltrane, J Dilla, Erykah Badu, Labi Siffre

Technological InfluencesRichard Boulanger, OKHOUSECAT, Matthew Davidson, Ajay Kapur, Ge Wang, Perry Cook, Tod Machover, Ian McCurdy, Max Matthews

B.A., Berklee College of Music, 2025
M.F.A., Creative Computing, California Institute of the Arts, expected 2027
