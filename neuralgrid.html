G2

The 12D State Manifold

10 spatial-like dimensions + 2 temporal dimensions

state axes (10)
real time — flows
probability-time — spreads

Real time flows forward, one tick at a time. Probability-time spreads: it is the fan of possible next-moments, explored by prediction and collapsed by action.

the player's actions are real-time collapses of probability space; the interface measures the collapse itself.

the player moves only here — DSP faders in G3 are the pushforward shadow of lawful 12D motion.

G3

The DSP Graph — 10 Axes, 4 Families

spectrum · excitation · time · space

Harmonicity is the anchor axis: within one note it reads as timbre, between notes it reads as harmony. Tuning is a runtime render table — never a dimension.

Example addresses (self-explanatory naming, illustrative not calibrated):

  • 808 kick — harmonic, sparse, dark, struck, mild NL, continuous, dry, pitch-down motion, center, point
  • FM bell — inharmonic, mid-density, bright, struck-decay, heavy FM, continuous, some spread, slow motion, off-center, near-point
  • supersaw — harmonic-dense, saturated, bright, sustained, mild NL, continuous, smeared, audio-rate detune, wide, enveloping
  • granular cloud — inharmonic/noisy, variable density, mid tilt, sustained texture, low NL, grains, heavily smeared, audio-rate modulated, wide, enveloping
