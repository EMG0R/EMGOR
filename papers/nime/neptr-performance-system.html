Performance System of a Modern Digital Luthier

Emory Smith

California Institute of the Arts

Draft — ICMC target · instrument running today

Abstract

NEPTR is the brain at the center of the performance system, linking audio interfaces, DIY controllers, and hyper-instruments with internal DSP and a graphical interface. It replaces the laptop, the synthesizer, and the pedalboard onstage. Csound runs on a Raspberry Pi 5 at 48 kHz with a 128-sample period — 6 ms from string to amplifier — carrying 63 effect modules across 20 menus, with a Python touchscreen interface mirrored over OSC and the Rust DEMIURGE supervisor underneath. This paper documents the system as a whole rather than the box alone: the operating layer beneath it, the effect architecture inside it, and the family of DIY satellites around it.
