BOUBA: An Intuitive System for Accessing the Benefits of Music Therapy

Emory Smith

EMGOR / California Institute of the Arts

Draft — NIME 2027 target

Abstract

BOUBA is a system providing tactile interaction with therapeutic generative synthesis. Designed for the non-musician seeking a psychologically beneficial relationship with sound, BOUBA targets four pillars of human experience: sleep, mood, productivity, and autonomic regulation. At reproduction price, its physical controls and self-contained state foster an intimate and unique relationship with the user, grounded in empirical research linking ambient consonance, rhythmic entrainment, and generative soundscapes to measurable physiological and psychological benefit.

Keywords: music therapy, generative synthesis, tangible interfaces, ChucK, Teensy, wellbeing

1. Introduction

Most therapeutic-sound products are apps: another screen, another subscription, another stream. BOUBA takes the opposite position — a small, self-contained physical object whose entire interface is a joystick, whose state lives nowhere but inside it, and whose sound is generated rather than played back. The bet is that physicality and self-containment are not packaging details but part of the therapeutic mechanism: an intimate, single-purpose object invites a different relationship with sound than a phone does.

2. Design

BOUBA runs on a Teensy 4.0 (subject to change) with no external connections besides power. A joystick is the primary control, selecting and interpolating between generative presets. One internal speaker sits onboard, with a battery for standalone portable use, while a physical aux output allows connection to high-fidelity speaker systems. An open-source web version mirrors the physical system, with both unified under ChucK for exact parity between online and physical.

The research baseline draws from all four pillars: white noise and sine-based music for sleep, natural soundscapes and consonant harmonics for mood, stochastic resonance and rhythmic entrainment for productivity, and ambient consonance for autonomic regulation. Each generative preset is an operationalization of one of these findings, and the joystick's interpolation space is the map between them.

3. Current Status

Design stage. BOUBA exists as the design above; a search of the EMGOR working archive found no dedicated BOUBA repository yet — its written sources are the 2026–2027 papers planning document and the site's papers overview. The ChucK-based generative practice it builds on is established elsewhere in the EMGOR universe (the CIESEN web instrument and the ChucK effect library in the code planets), and Teensy-based self-contained instruments such as LITE demonstrate the intended hardware form factor.

4. Future Work

Prototype the Teensy + joystick + speaker object; implement two presets per pillar in ChucK with the web mirror; then design a small user study around the four pillars with a music-therapy collaborator. Planned collaboration with Yahezkul (Berklee Music Therapy) and possibly Ajay. Target venues: AMTA and other music-therapy publications.
