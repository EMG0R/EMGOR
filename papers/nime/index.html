2026–2027 Season

  1. Workflow of a Modern Digital Luthier
    In submission — ROTURA · with Ajay Kapur
    The methodology. Embedded audio, fabrication, and interaction design as one workflow, argued through NEPTR and BOUBA.
  2. DemiurgeOS
    Running — Linux Audio Conference target · with Ilai Gilbert, Ajay Kapur
    The audio-first Pi OS under NEPTR — sync layer, MIDI pool, global clock, one config file.
  3. Performance System of a Modern Digital Luthier
    Running — ICMC target
    The instrument: Csound on a Pi 5, 63 effect modules across 20 menus, a Rust supervisor underneath, 6 ms string to amplifier.
  4. NeuralGrid
    Mid-build — NIME target · with Ajay Kapur, Jake Cheng
    A controller that speaks a symbolic vocabulary borrowed from tabla bols before it speaks sound, then translates through seven perceptual axes.
  5. Master’s Thesis — untitled
    In development — CalArts · with Ajay Kapur
    The binding argument. What the OS, the instrument, and the methodology mean together.
  6. BOUBA
    Built — TEI / DIS target
    A quadraphonic therapeutic sound object, roughly $80 to reproduce, plus a second prototype and a free web version.
  7. Integrating Neural Amp Modeling into Csound
    Complete — submitted · PDF + opcode source
    NAMProcess — real-time Neural Amp Modeler inference as a Csound opcode, with live dry-referenced volume compensation.
