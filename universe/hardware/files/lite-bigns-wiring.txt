BIGNS TEENSY 4.1 WIRING GUIDE
==============================

All pin numbers refer to Teensy 4.1 pins.
Solder everything to the NEW Teensy in the same positions.


ENCODER (rotary)
  Pin 6  --> Encoder A
  Pin 8  --> Encoder B
  Pin 2  --> Encoder Switch (button press)
             Other side of switch to GND

POTS (3 potentiometers)
  Pin 14 --> Pot 0 wiper  (Pitch)
  Pin 16 --> Pot 1 wiper  (Probability)
  Pin 17 --> Pot 2 wiper  (Master Volume)
             Each pot: one outer leg to 3.3V, other outer leg to GND

JOYSTICK (analog 2-axis + button)
  Pin 18 --> Joystick X axis
  Pin 19 --> Joystick Y axis
  Pin 3  --> Joystick Button
             Joystick VCC to 3.3V, GND to GND

ARCADE BUTTON
  Pin 4  --> Arcade button
             Other side to GND

AUDIO (I2S Quad output - Teensy Audio Shield or DAC)
  These are fixed Teensy 4.1 I2S pins:
  Pin 7  --> I2S LRCLK (WS)
  Pin 20 --> I2S BCLK (SCK)
  Pin 21 --> I2S TX (data out 1, channels 1+2)
  Pin 23 --> I2S TX (data out 2, channels 3+4)

NEOPIXELS
  Pin 5  --> 2x NeoPixel data (the small indicator LEDs)
             VCC to 3.3V (or Teensy 3.3V), GND to GND

  Pin 9  --> 25x NeoPixel strip data
             Strip VCC to EXTERNAL 5V supply (not Teensy!)
             Strip GND to EXTERNAL 5V supply GND
             External 5V supply GND also connects to Teensy GND

POWER
  USB     --> Powers the Teensy (do NOT send more than 5V to the 5V pin)
  GND     --> Common ground for everything (Teensy, pots, joystick,
              encoder, buttons, external 5V supply, NeoPixels)
  3.3V    --> Powers pots and joystick (comes from Teensy onboard reg)


GROUND BUS SUMMARY
  All these GNDs must be connected together:
    - Teensy GND
    - All pot legs (low side)
    - Joystick GND
    - Encoder switch
    - Arcade button
    - Small NeoPixel GND
    - NeoPixel strip GND
    - External 5V supply GND


PIN QUICK REFERENCE
  6   Encoder A
  8   Encoder B
  2   Encoder Switch
  3   Joystick Button
  4   Arcade Button
  5   NeoPixel (2 indicator LEDs)
  7   I2S LRCLK
  9   NeoPixel strip (25 LEDs)
  14  Pitch pot
  16  Probability pot
  17  Volume pot
  18  Joystick X
  19  Joystick Y
  20  I2S BCLK
  21  I2S TX1
  23  I2S TX2
