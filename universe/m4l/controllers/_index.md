# Controllers

Where the lab's Max for Live work meets physical hardware. Two lineages live here:

- **Infinity Gauntlet** — a wearable gesture controller (Arduino glove, serial into Live), through five hardware/firmware revisions.
- **GEORGE** — an FM synth voiced for an EWI wind controller, built for a collaborator.

Both are M4L devices on the Ableton side with custom hardware on the other end — the devices are useful reference even without the hardware, and the source folders carry the firmware.
