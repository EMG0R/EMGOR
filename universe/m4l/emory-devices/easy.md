# EASY v1.3

A MIDI chord engine that makes harmony one-finger easy. Play a single note; EASY builds the voicing — MAJ, MIN, DIM, AUG, DOM, MAJ7 qualities with altered extensions all the way out to b9/#11/b13 — and sends the full chord downstream. A transposable offset shifts the whole engine's register.

## Use

1. Drop `EASY_v1.3.amxd` onto a MIDI track, in front of any instrument.
2. Pick a chord quality and extensions.
3. Play roots with one finger; the instrument behind it receives the full stack.

## Install

Drag the `.amxd` onto a MIDI track before an instrument. Frozen device, ~85KB.
