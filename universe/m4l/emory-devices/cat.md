# CAT v1.5

Exactly what it sounds like. CAT opens a floating Jitter (`jit.world`) window with an animated cat whose animation speed is scaled from your Live set's tempo — roughly 60 to 250 BPM mapped onto playback rate, synced through the transport. Audio passes straight through the device.

Morale infrastructure. Every session needs one.

## Use

Drop it anywhere in any device chain. A floating window opens; the cat vibes at your tempo. Change the BPM, the cat keeps up.

## Install

Drag the `.amxd` onto a track. No dependencies, no audio processing, no calories.
