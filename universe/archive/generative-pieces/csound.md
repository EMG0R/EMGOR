# Nonlinear Daylight

A Csound study in nonlinear reverberation — no Cabbage UI, no plugin wrapper, just an orchestra that runs and makes a room that behaves wrongly on purpose.

It started life as a Raspberry Pi sketch, written to find out how much reverb a headless Pi could actually carry. It outgrew that question, and it lives here now with the rest of the Csound work rather than filed under hardware experiments.

The `.csd` is downloadable below and runs anywhere Csound does.
