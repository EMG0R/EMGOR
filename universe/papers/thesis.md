A practice-based master's thesis, written in the first person because the practitioner is the site of the inquiry. It binds three pieces of work that only mean something together: DemiurgeOS (the operating layer), the Performance System of a Modern Digital Luthier (the instrument), and the Workflow of a Modern Digital Luthier (the methodology).

Each paper alone is a system description. Together they are evidence for something larger: that the traditional sequence of digital instrument-making — specify, then program, then fabricate, then perform — has collapsed into a single iterative act that one practitioner can hold entirely in their head.
