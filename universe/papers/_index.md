# PAPERS

Research and writing from the EMGOR practice — CalArts Creative Computing.

The through-line: instruments, interfaces, and infrastructure built as one continuous act. Each paper documents a system that already exists in some form on the workbench — a pedal, a controller, a Pi image, an opcode — and asks what it means, what it measures, and who else can use it.

## Two orbits

**[2026–2027 SEASON](/papers/season-2026-27)** — the work that ships this academic year. Three papers and the thesis that binds them: the OS underneath everything, the instrument built on top of it, the methodology both are evidence for, and the master's thesis that argues the whole stack is one idea.

**[2027+](/papers/future)** — the queue. Work that is real but deferred: an opcode already written and submitted, a controller mid-build, an object finished and waiting for its paper, a protocol folding into the OS, and one concept still on paper.

The split is deliberate. A season you can finish is worth more than a list you can't.
