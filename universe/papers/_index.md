# PAPERS

Research and writing from the EMGOR practice — CalArts Creative Computing, 2026–2027.

The through-line: instruments, interfaces, and infrastructure built as one continuous act. Each paper documents a system that already exists in some form on the workbench — a pedal, a controller, a Pi image, an opcode — and asks what it means, what it measures, and who else can use it.

## Status

**Published / complete:**

- **Integrating Neural Amp Modeling into Csound** — the flagship. Submitted to ICSC 2026, PDF built, plugin shipped. Full paper and download live on this planet.

**Planned (2026–2027):**

1. **Unified System for Streaming Realtime Data** — one cable, all the data
2. **NeuralGrid** — a tactile interface for adaptive neural sequencing
3. **Open-Pedal** — DIY guitar pedals and synths against the price of DSP
4. **DemiurgeOS** — a Raspberry Pi operating layer for audio work
5. **NEPTR** — the performance brain that replaces the laptop onstage
6. **BOUBA** — tactile generative synthesis for music therapy
7. **Workflow of a Modern Digital Luthier** — the thesis that binds them

Each paper is a moon of this planet. Click through for the abstract, the state of the work, and — where it exists — the artifact itself.
