# NEPTR: A System for Performance and Improvisation

**Status: PLANNED** — target venues: NIME, ICMC. The plan is to *perform* with it at NIME and ICMC this year, and hopefully present it too.

## Abstract

*Formal abstract TBD.* NEPTR is the brain at the center of the performance system — linking audio interfaces, DIY controllers, and hyper-instruments with internal DSP and GUI. It replaces the laptop, the synthesizer, and the pedalboard onstage.

## The system

Dense sensors and controls onboard, with exposed I/O: three USB ports, an Ethernet port, and micro HDMI. Performance happens entirely through DIY controllers and DIY hyper-instruments — no laptop in sight.

The NEPTR performance system includes:

- Hyper-trumpet
- Hyper-guitar
- Hemi speaker(s)
- Wii remote
- DIY audio interface
