# EMGOR

Everything in this universe is real work — instruments, operating systems,
wearables, papers, patches, and songs — built by EMGOR (Emory Smith) and
published open source. Every planet is a project. Zoom in.

The source of truth is the working archive itself: what's here is synced
from the studio folders, additively, forever. Nothing is ever deleted.

- Grab any device, patch, or paper straight from its planet.
- Site + docs live in the open at [github.com/EMG0R/EMGOR](https://github.com/EMG0R/EMGOR).
