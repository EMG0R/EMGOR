# Samples

Foley one-shots recorded at home — mouth sounds, water bottles, toy animals, whatever was in reach. Every hit in these packs was performed and captured by EMGOR.

## Free mini-pack

Eight picks from the "home made" collection, downloadable right here:

- **BURP** — exactly what it says
- **CRICKET_STROKE** — ratcheted cricket toy, dragged
- **FROG_HIT** — wooden frog güiro, struck
- **KALMBA_NOTE** — kalimba single note, long tail
- **MOUTH_POP** — lip pop, tight transient
- **OWL_HIT** — ceramic owl whistle, percussive
- **SHMACK** — hand smack, mid-heavy
- **WATERBOTTLE_SHORT** — water bottle thump, short decay

## The rest of the collection

The full `home made/` folder holds ~40 more one-shots in source: AIRPODS, BOOK, BUZZ_POP, CLLLLL, COWBELL, CRICKET_HIT, DULL_TICK, FROG_STROKE, HARD_TICK, HMM, IPHONE, KALMBA_HIT, five KICK variants plus KICK_SHARP and KICK_SHORT, MEEPO, MELODICA, MOUTH_CLICK, MOUTHPIECE, OWL_BLOW, PEAK_POP, SHARP_TICK, four SNAREs, TING, VIBE, VWAP, a full WATERBOTTLE series, and a multisampled trumpet.

The parent `_samps/` folder adds processed kit pieces — 808 toms, hats, claps, glass, granular textures, whispering — kept in source only.
